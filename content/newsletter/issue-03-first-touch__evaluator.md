## Exercise check against Volume 6 §2 (passes)

The indicator is the quality touch: a touch that did what the player intended, judged honestly by the player. The exercise makes intent explicit before the ball arrives (the player calls left, right or forward, then plays the ball), so "did the touch do what I meant" is answered against a stated intent rather than reconstructed after the fact. The count excludes clean touches that went nowhere the player meant, which is the distinction the piece exists to make. The partner variation moves the call to the moment the ball leaves the passer's foot, so the body must be open before the direction is known; that trains the precondition of an intended touch (the open body shape) and mirrors the library drill's "zone called at delivery" rule. The progression returns intent to the player (decide during the look, judge afterwards), which is the unprompted end state Volume 6 §2 names. Both feet are covered. Passes.
