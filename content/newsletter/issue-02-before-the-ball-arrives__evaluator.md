## Verdict: REVISE (five local fixes applied in the same session, then PASS)

1. **Email subject length.** 67 characters vs the 50 bar. Email subject set to "What to watch instead of the scoreboard" (39); the long line stays as the web title.
2. **Preheader length.** 95 characters vs the 90 bar. Trimmed to "Scanning: what it is, how to spot it, and one way to train it this week." (71).
3. **Unevidenced norm.** "that is normal at thirteen, fourteen and fifteen" asserted an age norm for scan counts that canon does not give; Volume 6 §2 makes tracked indicators same-player comparisons only. Changed to "That is not a verdict; it is a starting point, and the only comparison that means anything is with the same player later."
4. **Parent-boundary guard missing.** A parent counting scans from the sideline risks turning the count into sideline commentary and drive-home judgement, which Volume 7 warns against and which would convert a development indicator into pressure. Added: "Keep it to yourself unless the player asks; the point is to change what you watch, not what you say on the drive home."
5. **Exercise did not train the habit as defined.** The two-person cues (fingers behind the passer's back, a mouthed word) required looking at the passer, which is looking toward the ball's origin, not away from it. Rewritten: two-person version (passer raises an arm as the ball leaves the foot; receiver calls left or right before the first touch) trains eyes-off-the-ball in flight; three-person version puts the cue behind or beside the receiver so the look is over the shoulder, which matches the Volume 6 §2 definition.

## Grounding check (passes after revision)

- Scan definition and counting convention: Volume 6 §2 tracked indicators (deliberate look away from the ball before receiving, counted per reception, prompted scans count first, goal unprompted). Reproduced as practice guidance in our own words, not as Codex text.
- Same-player consistency, never cross-player comparison: Volume 6 §2.
- "Record opportunities as well as actions" (a player who received four times has had few chances): Volume 6 §2 observed category.
- Scanning as a trainable habit rather than a gift: Conviction 9.
- Process before outcome framing carried over from issue #1: Conviction 5.
- No research statistics quoted; "one of the very few numbers ... that tells you something about development" is a claim about our indicator set, not a measured rate.

## Anti-pattern check (passes)

Title honestly represents content; no fearmongering (the low count is framed as a starting point, not a deficit); no talent labelling ("attention rather than talent" is the reverse); no results-only framing; no urgency, scarcity or sales pressure; CTA is the free drill library; no belt or level language; no em-dashes.
